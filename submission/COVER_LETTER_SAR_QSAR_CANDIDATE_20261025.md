# Cover letter candidate for SAR and QSAR in Environmental Research

> Candidate only. Final author, declaration, repository and portal checks remain required.

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, “An Auditable Reliability-Regret Profile for Retrospective ADMET QSAR Model Selection: A Fixed-Benchmark Study Across 22 Endpoints,” for publication as a Research Article in *SAR and QSAR in Environmental Research*.

The manuscript addresses a practical evaluation problem in QSAR-oriented ADMET modeling: a family selected by one validation metric may not be the family that behaves most reliably across calibration, selective risk, residual coverage and chemical-support dimensions. We define a reliability-regret profile (RRP) as an endpoint-by-dimension audit estimand. It compares the validation-selected family with a retrospective reference formed from the eligible candidate pool on the fixed held-out partition, while explicitly marking that reference as non-deployable.

The primary audit covers 22 TDC ADMET endpoints, five model families and five fitted seeds. The evidence contract records sample identities, split roles, seed aggregation, prediction artifacts, provenance and claim boundaries. Validation and reliability winners differed across the prespecified reliability dimensions, with the largest mismatch fractions observed for selective prediction and applicability alignment. The manuscript also reports calibration and support-conditioned diagnostics with endpoint-level uncertainty and sensitivity analyses.

We position the work conservatively alongside recent studies of applicability-domain reliability, uncertainty quantification under ADME data shifts, TDC reproducibility and leakage, and the OpenADMET-ExpansionRx blind challenge. Those studies establish important neighboring results; our manuscript does not claim a new uncertainty estimator, calibration method, applicability-domain method, temporal split, or reproducibility audit. Its contribution is the explicit reliability-regret selection audit and the associated evidence contract.

The analysis is retrospective and bounded. The planned broad confirmatory external matrix was held after its outcome-blind power gate failed. The focused CYP2D6 analysis reuses previously examined source outcomes and is reported as exploratory because the test-role outcomes were inspected before the focused protocol freeze. The manuscript does not claim independent external validation, temporal validation, deployment effectiveness, clinical utility, or superiority over recent comparator studies.

Huajian Jiang is the sole, first and corresponding author. No funding was received; the manuscript states no competing interests, no acknowledgements, and that ethics approval and consent are not applicable to this public-data computational study. The work is original and is not under consideration elsewhere.
