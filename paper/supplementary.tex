\section{Locked experiment matrix}
The primary matrix contains 22 endpoints, five model families, and five seeds
(1--5), for 550 fit slots. The model families are random forest, XGBoost,
LightGBM, GIN, and Chemprop D-MPNN. The cleaned-data sensitivity matrix contains
120 additional fits and is analyzed separately. The descriptive external
transport diagnostics contain 120 traditional-model fits, 20 GIN inference
files, and 10 Chemprop inference files; these descriptive files are not counted
as broad confirmatory external validation. A separate narrow CYP2D6 rerun is
reported in the main manuscript and kept in its own audited evidence ledger.

\section{Validation-only ExpansionRx replication}
The post-hoc ExpansionRx replication contains eight regression endpoints, five
candidate model families, and five seeds per family (200 model slots). The
validation-reliability selector (VRRS) combines equal within-endpoint rank scores
for validation MAE and support/error Spearman correlation. The selector protocol
was written after execution and is therefore not preregistered, but the selection
table and selector freeze were completed before any test-target directory was
opened. The selector map and the validation-MAE-only comparator are:

The VRRS choice differed from the MAE-only choice on 6/8 endpoints. Leave-one-
seed stability agreed with the full five-seed VRRS choice in 33/40 comparisons
(82.5\%). This is a validation-stage stability diagnostic from a post-hoc rule;
no ExpansionRx test targets were read, and no unseen-test improvement or
independent external validation is claimed.

\section{Primary reliability dimensions}
\begin{longtable}{p{0.29\linewidth}p{0.62\linewidth}}
\toprule
Dimension & Locked definition and interpretation \\
\midrule
ECE & Expected calibration error over ten equal-width probability bins for binary tasks. \\
Brier & Mean squared probabilistic error for binary tasks. \\
Coverage error & Absolute deviation from nominal 90\% residual-interval coverage for regression tasks; validation residuals are used, so this is a diagnostic rather than an independent conformal guarantee. \\
Relative excess AURC & Risk-coverage area above a loss-ordered descriptive oracle, normalized by mean sample loss. \\
Applicability alignment & Spearman association between maximum Morgan/Tanimoto support and negative absolute prediction error. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Evidence and limitations}
The retained core-matrix release gate reports 550/550 exact run keys and status
\texttt{GO}. The formal CPU ledger was executed in the tracked remote research
environment; the local Mac replay of the 330 traditional-model artifacts is a
separate verification lane. The broad confirmatory external-cohort matrix
remains unstarted because its outcome-blind power screen is
\texttt{POWER\_FAIL\_HOLD}; the planned matrix therefore remains
\texttt{0/150}, not a completed confirmatory result. A separate narrow CYP2D6
rerun completed 10/10 prediction packages, but it is exploratory only:
test-role metrics were examined before protocol freeze, and all ten rerun
predictions were byte-identical to the earlier candidate outputs. Hash and
arithmetic checks do not repair this prior exposure. The CYP2D6 diagnostic is
excluded from confirmatory evidence and is limited to that endpoint and its two
source cohorts. Raw TDC
rows and model checkpoints are not redistributed in the
public source package, so sanitized prediction views alone cannot reproduce
label-dependent metrics.

\section{Runtime environment and literature boundary}
The verified formal neural environment was Linux x86\_64 with an NVIDIA A40,
CUDA 12.8, Python 3.12.10, Chemprop 2.2.1, PyTorch 2.7.1+cu128, RDKit
2026.09.1, and NumPy 2.5.4. The independent Mac replay used macOS 26.6 arm64,
Python 3.11.15, scikit-learn 1.9.1, RDKit 2026.09.1, XGBoost 3.2.0, and
LightGBM 4.7.0. The exact package inventory of the formal remote traditional
CPU lane was not preserved in the local manifests and is not inferred. The
full inventory and lane boundary are recorded in
the repository environment inventory document.

The closest cited studies differ in estimand. Zhao et al. evaluate
challenge-specific ADMET reliability scenarios; Kamuntavičius et al. study
representation/optimization and cross-source transfer; Zheng et al. study a
structural-frontier split against a matched scaffold control. The present work
freezes one audited TDC snapshot and reports validation-selection versus
multi-dimensional held-out reliability regret. The comparison audit is
recorded in the repository comparison audit.
